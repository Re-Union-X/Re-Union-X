# Coliseum Part 3b (2018-04 .. 2019-04) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the thirteen WW Coliseum seasons 2018-04 .. 2019-04 (the lump-sum era) served on top of the Part 1-3 mechanism:
lump-sum tier coins, 99-round skips, Broom / Fantasia Mickey B / Moon Gem tier rewards, the 2018-12 structure (no per-round
coins, Coliseum Board + Tier 11 / 12 / 13 Bonus boards, 9 / 8 / 12 ranking brackets), boards with their cards, ranking prizes
and thresholds calibrated on this era's cutoffs.

**Architecture:** no new mechanism and no generated map. `make-colosseum.js` takes 13 more ReLux seasons, maps the ReLux
medal ids of the tier rewards to ours, accepts per-season bracket lists instead of `BRACKETS8`, and resolves the new enemy ids
through the Part 3 source order. `import_colosseum_stages.py` rebuilds the 5 missing JP STG headers from their part file.
Part 4 (2019-05 .. 2021-06, the revamp) follows this plan.

**Tech Stack:** Node (`node server/test-*.js`), Python 3 tools in `recon/tools/` (`python -I`), PowerShell session scripts.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-coliseum-design.md`. Research (private, local) in
`private\events\sources\coliseum\`: `PART3_AUDIT.md` §1-§6 (2018-04 .. 12 rows), `PART4_AUDIT.md` §1a, §3, §4a, §5, §7
(2019-01 .. 04), `ENEMIES_2018.md` (enemy census of the 13 sets, written before Task 3), `CUTOFFS_2018.md` (border ranks,
written before Task 5), `board_art_all.json` (cards of all 44 boards, prepared and approved), COLISEUM.md, RULES.md; ReLux
extracts `private\donations\relux_2026-11\verified\` and the raw enemy table of `DEFAULT_RELUX_ENEMY`. Part 3 plan, ledger and
summary: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-26-coliseum-part3.md`, `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-26-coliseum-part3/progress.md`,
`docs/notes/coliseum.md` («Part 3 summary», «Follow-ups for the next part»).

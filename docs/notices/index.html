Differences from the original game

Re-Union X works completely offline: no other players and nothing is sold.

Your Story is your calendar: each Story period lasts several weeks, and Shop, Raids, Events and Special Quests show one week of it at a time.

Overview

  • Re-Union X is a fan restoration that works completely offline: there are no other players and nothing is sold. Some rules of the original game had to change. Here they are in short; each topic has its own notice with every detail.

📅 Your Story is your calendar

  • How far you are in the Story decides which part of the original game (2016-2021) you are playing in. Each group of Story Quests matches a period of the original calendar, and a period usually lasts several weeks.
  • The Shop, Raids, Events and Special Quests show one week of your period at a time: what the original game offered in that week.
  • Every Monday (08:00 UTC) they move on to the next week of the same period. After the last week of the period they start again from its first week, so nothing is lost.
  • Example: your highest cleared Story Quest is between 451 and 475. That is the period from October 13 to November 9, 2016: 4 weeks.
  • The first week you see October 13-19, 2016; the next Monday October 20-26; then October 27 - November 2; then November 3-9; then October 13-19 again.
  • Clear Story Quest 476 to reach the next period (from November 10, 2016), with its new banners, raids and events.
  • Seasonal banners (New Year, Halloween, Holidays...) follow the real calendar instead.

🔄 Resets (UTC)

  • Every day at 08:00: Daily Missions, Login Bonus, the free draw of the day.
  • Every Monday at 08:00: Weekly Missions and the new week of the Shop, Raids, Events and Special Quests. The game downloads updated data: the first loading takes longer.
  • On the 1st of each month at 08:00: the monthly Lux campaign and a data download. Raid Boss levels go back to LV 1 at the start of the 1st (00:00 Pacific time).

📌 Catalogs: keep one of each

  • Shop, Special, Raids and Events each have a Catalog as their first banner: tap Details to see everything of the original game.
  • From a Catalog you can keep one past banner, quest, raid or event: it stays available every week. Keeping another one replaces it.
  • Only things from a period your Story has already passed can be kept. Leave and re-enter the screen to see it.

⚔️ Raids

  • Raids are solo for now. A boss you do not defeat keeps the damage you dealt until you beat it or it escapes.
  • Monday to Friday: raids cost their original AP. Saturday and Sunday (Pacific time): 0 AP and Bonus Time all day with double Lux.
  • Every raid is open all day. A boss gains 1 level per defeat, up to LV 99. A kept raid has a level of its own, from LV 1.

🎉 Events

  • Events that lasted only hours are open for their whole original day.
  • Event Coins never expire. Event quests never bring a Raid Boss.

💎 Shop and Jewels

  • No purchases: Jewels come from quests, missions, the Login Bonus and Presents.
  • All Free Deals share one free draw a day.
  • Draw rates that the original game never published are REBUILT: each banner's Details say so.

👑 VIP

  • VIP is no longer a paid pass: complete all the Weekly Missions of a week to get it for the rest of that week and the whole next one.
  • For now it cannot be earned: the Weekly Missions include Union Cross, which is not back yet.

🎁 Nothing is lost

  • Campaign rewards start when your Story reaches them and never end.
  • Event Coins and Draw Points never expire. Mission rewards you forgot to collect go to your Presents.

🧩 Reconstructed parts

  • Where the original data or graphics are lost, they are rebuilt and marked REBUILT in the notices. The full list is in Differences in detail.

Differences in detail

Every rule that differs from the original game, topic by topic.

No purchases, and presents never expire NOT ORIGINAL.

Game and progress

  • Re-Union X is a fan restoration of KINGDOM HEARTS Union χ[Cross] that works completely offline: the small server the game talks to runs on your own phone or computer, nothing goes online, and your progress is saved only there.
  • The in-game date is set between 2016 and 2021 so that the original game keeps working. Missions reset at the real times shown in their countdowns.
  • Completed missions that you did not collect before they end are sent to your Presents.
  • The maximum LV is 999, as in the original game from January 2021. The Lux needed for each level up to LV 304 is original; from LV 305 it is REBUILT from players’ videos of 2018-2021, because the original values are lost.
  • Limited Missions rotate among the original campaigns: one monthly Lux campaign and one short campaign each week.

Medals

  • A few Spirit parts without original graphics are replaced by a similar part.
  • The "Medal STR +N" and "Medal DEF +N" effects of Special Attacks and Supernovas are rebuilt from their descriptions, because the original values are lost.
  • The Art button in a Medal's details shows the original full artwork of the 55 Medals that had one: on every star of those families, on the SN++ reissues with the same picture (Key Art #3, Key Art #8, Axel Art, Roxas Art) and on their boosted copies.
  • Medals with a different picture, such as SN++ - Terra, Ven, Aqua, have no Art button.
  • Reconstructed: these Medals are part of an artwork and show it, although the original data gave them none: Illustrated Aqua (A, SN, SN+), Illustrated Xemnas, Sora B, Terra B, Donald B, Goofy, Marluxia [EX], Xion [EX], KH II Kairi [EX+], Supernova HD Terra, HD Ventus and HD Aqua, and the SN++ Illustrated Xemnas, Illus. Marluxia, Illustrated Zexion and Master Xehanort.

Raid Boards

  • The Sora & Moogle B Medal (JP 5th anniversary, a Raid Board reward) is REBUILT from its description: used in a Level Up it adds 100 STR and 100 DEF, like Chip and Dale.
  • The Raid Board cell listed as a 6★ Fantasia Mickey A gives the 5★ Fantasia Mickey A: no 6★ version of that Medal is known, and the board pairs it with Magic Brooms, which evolve the 5★.
  • The Starlight Ears and Dazzling Disco Spirit parts (Raid Board rewards) each have one REBUILT texture: a small twinkling sparkle and a tiny dot were lost and are drawn with the matching sparkle of the same set.

Shop and draws

  • Shop draw rates are reconstructed where the original game did not publish them (Medal rarities, guarantees and Skill rates are original): Draw Odds and each banner's Details say so.
  • Bonus items of a draw (Magic Mirror, Gems...) arrive in your Presents.
  • All Free Deals share one draw a day, and VIP Free Deals need the VIP benefit (see the Free Deals and VIP Draws notices).
  • Free deals that could be drawn only once (or once per day) keep that limit.
  • Seasonal draw currencies (Spooky Cookies, Moogle Points, Holiday Draw Tickets) are kept separately; the Shop shows one of them at a time. When you earn one of them, the reward message calls it a Draw Ticket, but it goes to its own currency.
  • Some 2016-17 banners only drew Epic Medals; the game data no longer says which Medals were Epic, so these banners draw from their full sets.
  • Blue Fairy Deal banners are in the Banner Catalog but cannot be drawn yet: Blue Fairies will come with a future update.
  • Trait Medals are back: a deal that guaranteed a Trait Medal gives it (all 313 Trait Medals, matched to their Medals from the community wiki).
  • Boosted Medals: the chance of a boosted version on deals that offered one is reconstructed (5% per eligible Medal). Guaranteed max-level Skills are given only for Skills that exist in this restoration.
  • Boosted stats on deals that did not state them are reconstructed (+1000 STR, +1000 DEF), and bonus support Medals earned through a guarantee arrive in your Presents.

Seasonal and kept banners

  • Seasonal banners follow fixed dates of the real calendar, and the Shop shows the most recent unlocked edition of each season, from the first time you log in inside its window. In the original game each edition ran on its own dates.
  • The banner you keep in the Shop with Keep in the Shop did not exist in the original game.
  • Limited Missions that give Platinum Tickets or Draw Tickets appear only after a banner that uses them is unlocked.
  • Draw Points from selling Medals start only when the Story reaches the first Draw Points Deal, and they never expire (see the Draw Points notice).

Home banners

  • The banners on the Home screen show the current Shop deals and the events, raids and Special Quests of your Story week, with the time left. Tap one to open it in the Shop or in Quests. The original lists of these banners did not survive, so which banners appear and in what order is chosen by this server NOT ORIGINAL.

Purchases

  • There are no purchases: Jewels and items come from quests, missions and presents.
  • The Present Box holds up to 500 presents and 500 Jewel presents, where the original held 200 NOT ORIGINAL: campaigns that start when your Story reaches them can send many presents at once. Presents are never deleted: not for being over the limit, and they never expire, where the original deleted them after 30 days NOT ORIGINAL. The expiry date shown on each present is only a placeholder far in the future.
  • Spirit Training slots that were bought in the original game open in other ways NOT ORIGINAL:
  • Slot 2 ("Purchase Weekly Jewels"): open while you have the VIP benefit of the Weekly Missions.
  • Slot 4 ("Purchase Jewel Box E"): open once you complete all the monthly Limited Missions (the month's Lux campaign), for the rest of that month and the whole next one, although its label says "Valid until the end of the month".
  • Slot 3 keeps its original rule: 5 draws from 10-Medal Deals paid with Jewels (not Free Deals or Ticket Draws) open it for 3 days.

Coliseum

The monthly Coliseum seasons are back from Story Quest 130: April 2016 to March 2018 for now.

The season follows your Story week; each season keeps its own progress and coins NOT ORIGINAL.

Ranking brackets became score thresholds; keep one past season from the Coliseum Catalog.

Overview24 seasons, from Story Quest 130

The Coliseum is back in Quests from Story Quest 130: the monthly seasons of the Olympus arena with their Tiers and Rounds, the original enemies, the one-turn shortcut, Coliseum Coins, Tier Rewards, the Coliseum Boards and the ranking rewards. Twenty-four seasons are here: April 2016 to March 2018.

Seasonsfollow your Story week, progress kept

The season follows your Story like the Shop: the Coliseum plays the season of the month of your Story week NOT ORIGINAL (the original changed season on the 1st of every real month). A week that spans two months counts for the month it starts in, and when your Story week goes back to an earlier month the Coliseum goes back to that season too. In a Story week whose month has no season here yet, the Coliseum is unavailable.

Each season keeps its own progress, Coliseum Coins and Coliseum Boards in your save NOT ORIGINAL. In the original game every new month started from Tier 1 with 0 coins and the coins of the old season were lost; here, going back to a season resumes it where you left it. A season played for the first time starts at Tier 1, Round 1 with 0 coins, as in the original.

From November 2016 a season has eleven Tiers, one more than before, as in the original: Tier 10 has 10 rounds and Tier 11 is the endless one.

Every season has all the Tiers of the original data: from January 2017 some Tiers have 999 rounds before the endless last one (Tiers 11-14 from January to March 2017, 11-20 in April 2017). A save in the old last Tier keeps its Tier and Round.

From May 2017 a season has 22 Tiers, as in the original: Tiers 11, 12 and 13 have 50, 100 and 300 rounds, then nine Tiers of 999 rounds, the last one endless.

Rounds and APretry freely, original AP campaigns

As in the original game, a lost or abandoned round can be tried again as often as you like, and the AP paid to start it is not given back. Clearing every wave in one turn unlocks the next rounds at once, also across tiers; when you clear a later round, the rounds and tiers you skipped pay their coins and Tier Rewards.

Rounds cost their original 3 to 5 AP. The Coliseum AP campaigns of 2016 are back on their original days of your Story week: 1 AP from May 19 to 31 and from June 16 to 22, 2016, and 0 AP! on November 1-3, 2016, for the Coliseum season being played: the November 2016 season, unless you keep another one.

From May 2017 every round costs 0 AP, as in the original data.

The «1 AP» rate is REBUILT: this version of the game rounds the original rate up to 2 AP on a round, so the rate is adjusted to keep 1 AP.

Rankingbrackets became score thresholds

There is only one player on this server, so the monthly ranking cannot be a real ranking. Each reward bracket of a season is a score threshold instead NOT ORIGINAL, on the original measure: the furthest round you cleared. Reach a threshold and its rewards go to your Present Box, once; the Ranking screen lists the thresholds.

The thresholds are REBUILT: the only worldwide cutoffs on record are those of August 2016 (recordings of its ranking). August to October 2016 use values read from them; the other seasons are interpolated from them and adjusted to their number of rounds (from November 2016, ten more rounds: every threshold is 10 rounds higher).

From May 2017 each month uses the border ranks posted by players at its end (r/KingdomHearts, 2017): its thresholds follow those values. August 2017 and January to March 2018 have none and use the curve of the whole 2017 era.

About 100,000 players a month were ranked in 2017, so almost everyone was in the top 100,000: from May 2017 the 50,001st-100,000th bracket and the lower ones need only 1 round.

The rewards of each bracket are the original ones: from the game data and the notices of their season up to November 2016, from the community wiki from December 2016. The December 2016 Pegasus Knight Helm goes to the top 10,000, as its notice says. The December 2017 Santa Hat, Beard and Brows are Spirit parts, as its notice says.

In May 2016 every ranked player got the same reward, as in the original: one cleared round is enough. Before your first round of May 2016 the goal reads 10000000th: that was the original lowest bracket of that season (5,000,001st to 10,000,000th).

Coliseum Catalogkeep one past season, filters

The Coliseum Catalog is NOT ORIGINAL: it is an addition of this server, like the Event and Raid Catalogs. Its banner is the first of the Events tab and appears from Story Quest 130, when the Coliseum opens; tap it to open the Training Grounds, and its Details button lists every season here with its tiers, AP, Coliseum Boards (with their cards) and your progress. Tap a season for its page: its Coliseum Boards with the nodes unlocked and the coins still needed, its Tiers and Tier Rewards, the score thresholds and where its maps come from.

One past season at a time can be kept: the Coliseum then plays the kept season until you remove it, whatever your Story week, also when your Story week goes back to an earlier month. Only past seasons can be kept: not the season of your Story week, nor a month your Story week has only just reached. Keeping or removing a season loses nothing, since each season keeps its progress and coins. A kept season shows «Ends in» the end of the current week NOT ORIGINAL: it never ends on its own, but the screen needs a date.

The Catalog year tabs and Filters NOT ORIGINAL find seasons by game year, by month or board name, by state (season of your Story, can be kept now, kept, later in the Story), by Coliseum Boards all completed or not, and by Tier Rewards all taken or not.

The Coliseum Catalog banner and the season cards are our own design NOT ORIGINAL, made from original pieces of the game: the Olympus arena panel, the Coliseum Coin and the Tier crown.

Mapsoriginal, Japanese and generated maps

The maps of April to July 2016 are the original ones, from the 2016 worldwide game files. December 2016 to April 2017, May 2017 and January to March 2018 use the original maps of the Japanese version, which the worldwide game most likely reused (its round lists match them).

No map of August to November 2016 and June to December 2017 survives: their rounds are played on generated maps NOT ORIGINAL, built from the round lists of the community wiki (khuxwiki) with the original enemies of those seasons, in the original arena.

The wiki lists enemy buffs (higher Strength or Defense) on some waves of the maps 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 10 of August 2016, 1 and 7 of September 2016, and on most maps of June to December 2017.

The ten maps of a season repeat in every tier: rounds 1, 11, 21 … of a tier play map 1, rounds 2, 12, 22 … map 2, and so on.

A map cannot carry those buffs, so these enemies fight without them NOT ORIGINAL; the counter of the June 2017 Ringmaster is dropped too.

The wiki gives only the name of the boss of map 10 of June 2017 and September 2017: they are the Coliseum Ringmaster and Enraged Elk of the game data.

On the eighth map of November 2016, three Shadows of the third wave stand on places added by the generator NOT ORIGINAL: no original layout holds seven enemies in one wave. Eight maps of 2017 have one or two such places.

Enemiesoriginal rows, a few by the rule

The Coliseum enemies of 2017 and 2018 have their original stats: from the original Coliseum enemy table (ReLux Team donation), or a copy of the original row when a season reuses an enemy under the id of its month.

Where no original row survives (the June 2017 Ringmaster among them), the row is REBUILT: derived from its original base enemy by the variant rule of the original Coliseum rows (same moves, HP raised from level 300). No value is estimated.

Graphicsboard cards, one button icon stands in

The card pictures of the Coliseum Boards are original art: from the 2016 worldwide files or the Japanese files where they match the board, else the board previews of the community wiki at their original size.

The cards of the Mango Sweetie (July 2017) and Illusionist (September 2017) boards are REBUILT from the small picture of the community wiki, the only one that survives.

The Puppy Medium and Puppy Bob boards were in the Japanese version only: they are not here.

The icon of the Coliseum Boards button is not in any surviving game file: the Multi Board icon stands in for it NOT ORIGINAL.

Not here yetlater seasons and PvP

The seasons from April 2018 to June 2021 will come in later updates. The PvP Coliseum is not available yet.

Event Quests: how they work

The original Event Quests are back in Quests > Events, following your Story week.

Rewards are given once, drops keep their original chances, and Event Coins never expire.

The Event Catalog lists every event; keep one past event at a time.

552 events · 4,256 of 4,256 quests · 115 Event Boards
Overviewpast events following your Story week

The Event Quests are back: the limited-time events of the original game, with their original maps, enemies, objectives and rewards wherever their files survive. They are in Quests > Events, after the raids.

Events follow your Story like the Shop: each week shows the events whose original dates fall in the matching week of the original game. An event that ran for three weeks is listed in each of those weeks.

Today 552 events are served, with 4256 of their 4256 quests (583 events are in the data so far, from July 2016 to February 2021). The others follow in later updates: the Event Catalog lists every event and, for each quest not served yet, the reason (a map or an enemy whose original files are still missing). A quest whose enemy is missing is not served: no other enemy stands in for it.

Opening hoursopen for the whole original day

An event that was open for only a few minutes or hours in the original game is open here for its whole original day NOT ORIGINAL: it is listed on the real weekday (Pacific time) of that day, all day. An event with one quest a day lists all its quests for its whole period (NOT ORIGINAL where the original opened them one a day).

Event Cataloglist every event, keep one past event

The Event Catalog is NOT ORIGINAL: it is an addition of this server, in the style of the Special and Raid Catalogs. Its banner in the Events tab opens the Training Grounds when tapped, and its Details button lists every event of the game with its quests.

One past event at a time can be kept in the Events tab (it shows the KEPT EVENT tab), beside the events of the week. Events later than your Story stay locked (Unlocks at Story Quest N), and events with no quest served yet are marked Not built yet and cannot be kept.

Rewardsonce, original drop chances

Objective rewards and first-clear items are given once, as in the original game. Enemy drops keep their original chances: an item that dropped 5% of the time still does.

For Event Coins, see Event coins and boards below. Each event's Details shows its original notice as it was.

Event quests never bring a Raid Boss and do not fill the raid gauge. This is the original rule from January 24, 2019, when Raid Bosses began to appear only in raid events; before that date some event quests could bring one, so for older events it is NOT ORIGINAL.

The story scenes before and after some event quests are not shown yet.

Event coins and boardscoins never expire

Event Coins are back, with the Event Boards: today 115 Event Boards in 23 board sets, for 51 events. The quest list of these events has the Event Boards button. Event Coins come from objectives (once) and from enemy drops (at every clear, also for a kept event).

Each board set (the boards of a month, shared by its events) has one balance, and Event Coins never expire NOT ORIGINAL (the original game lost them when the event ended, as the board screen still says). Each cell and each finished board gives its reward once.

One board set is listed at a time: the set of the last event quest you started (only a quest of an event whose Event Boards are available counts), otherwise the newest set of your Story week, otherwise the set of your kept event. The Event Coins shown on the boards are that set's balance; the other sets keep theirs.

In the original game every cell of an Event Board paid a reward, and here every served board pays them all, the Booster cells included (see Boosters below). A board with a cell that cannot be paid here is not served, nor is a board that opens after it. The sets of November 2016 and March 2017 are not served yet (3 events): those events are served without Event Boards, and the Event Coins they pay are kept in the balance of their set, which is not shown until its boards arrive.

Raid Coins are unchanged: raids pay and spend them as before.

Announcement notices with no quest and no banner of their own (for example «New Event Coins Available in October!») are not events: the Event Catalog does not list them, and their boards are on the pages of the events of their set.

The Riku & Moogle 6★ Medal, a drop of the August and September 2019 coin events, is back: a Sell Medal with no stats, sold for 500 Jewels, with its original picture.

Boostersrebuilt effects, timed from pickup

The Boosters are back: 428 Boosters, paid by 49 event quests, 267 Event Board cells, 3 Raid Board cells and 79 Avatar Board cells. As in the original game, a Booster raises the damage of the Medals it names, or the slot multipliers of the Keyblade it names, until it expires: you see it in your profile (Boosters, with its expiry), as the light-blue +N% plate on the Medal Details screen, and in battle. Different Boosters add up.

No original Booster record with its effect values survives: each effect is rebuilt from the Booster's original description (the attribute, Upright or Reversed, the group of Medals or the Keyblade it names, and its value). The Medals of a group are the ones the original game data assigns to that Booster.

In the original every Booster expired on a fixed date. Here a Booster lasts, from the moment you get it, as many days as it lasted in the original from the start of its event to its expiry (8 to 32 days) NOT ORIGINAL (in the original a Booster obtained late in its event lasted less). So a Booster from a past event, a kept event or an older board set works for its full time too. For 156 Boosters whose expiry notice did not survive, the end of their event or board is used as the expiry.

Each quest objective and each board cell gives its Booster once, ever: a replay does not give it again, not even after it expired. The same Booster from another quest or cell extends the one you have.

A Booster reward recorded before the Boosters arrived (a quest you cleared earlier) is given at your first login after this update, with its full time.

Every original banner is in the Banner Catalog. Banners unlock as you progress through the Story; in the original game they followed the calendar.

Weekly rotationone week of your Story period

Each part of the Story matches a period of the original calendar. A period usually lasts several weeks (an example is in the notice «Differences from the original game»). The Shop shows one week of that period at a time: every banner that ran during that week, up to 20.

Every Monday the Shop moves to the next week; after the last week of the period it starts again from the first one, so every banner of your period comes back.

When you clear enough Story Quests to reach the next period, the Shop moves to the first week of the new period. Banners of periods you have passed stay in the Banner Catalog, where you can still see their Medals and rates.

Each banner's Details say whether it is in the Shop now and, if not, in how many weeks it comes back.

Seasonal bannersreal-calendar windows

During each of these windows of the real calendar, the most recent unlocked edition of that season's banners appears at the top of the Shop, right after the Banner Catalog (and the kept banner, if any), from the first time you log in inside the window until the first login after it ends. If no edition is unlocked yet, nothing appears.

Anniversary: April 6 – April 30

When two seasons overlap, both appear, unless they use different special tickets (Spooky Cookies, Moogle Points, Holiday Draw Tickets): then only the first one in the list above shows. A seasonal banner that is already in the week's rotation is not shown twice.

Keep one banner in the Shopone past banner stays every week

In the Banner Catalog, tap 📌 Keep in the Shop on a banner's row (or in its Details). It then stays in the Shop every week, right after the Banner Catalog plate and above the seasonal banners, marked with a 📌 KEPT IN SHOP badge.

Leave and re-enter the Shop after keeping a banner to see it.
